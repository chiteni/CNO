PADDLE PARTY
Open index.html in a modern browser. No installation or internet required.
Keep the script folder beside index.html.

Player 1: W and S. Player 2: Up and Down arrows.
Space: serve at the beginning and after every point.
First to 5 wins. Use New match to restart.
Touch controls appear on touch devices.
The supplied engine also supports P to pause and C to continue.

gameEngine.js is an exact unchanged copy of your supplied engine.
All new game sprites and behaviour are in script/myCode.js.
The page appearance is in index.html.
